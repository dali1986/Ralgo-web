            How it works

            The work asks one of its questions. One voice, chosen at random from thirteen, answers beside it, and you rate the answer with stars before the next question comes. Voices people rate well are chosen more often. Sage is the thirteenth voice: a blend of the other twelve, weighted by their ratings, and it is the voice that answers when you ask it something yourself. In the questioners, Sage talks with Anti-Sage, its opposite, made from the same twelve voices weighted by how poorly they are rated. The work asks one of its questions, and the two take it from there with no one steering: each replies to everything the other just said and ends with a question of its own, then its reply scrolls up for the other to answer. Anti-Sage speaks in the opposite colour.
