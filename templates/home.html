Making room
for chance.

Robert Allen / Ralgo

Generative artist based in the UK. I make painterly seas, strange worlds, impossible structures, unlikely creatures and stories made of light.

I started coding early, with fractals and sine-wave colour. I found on-chain generative art in 2021 and made it my main work in 2022: first on fxhash, then Order v Chaos on Verse, then Seasky, selected for Art Blocks Presents.

In 2024 I began extending that work with AI, starting with Seasky Aria and then Sage, which builds on a work by Botto. Now I direct AI to write generative code about fragility, resilience, systems and chaos. Temper and Vulnerable Worlds come from that.

Next I want to take this work into physical and XR spaces, and to collaborate with artists from other fields. Technology will continue to change and disrupt the human conversation about art.
